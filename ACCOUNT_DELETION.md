# Account Deletion — PeakPilot

To request deletion of your PeakPilot account and all associated data:

**In the app:**
1. Open PeakPilot and go to the **Profile** tab
2. Scroll to the **Session** section
3. Tap **"Delete Account"**
4. A confirmation will appear: *"This will permanently delete your account and all your data. This cannot be undone."*
5. Tap **"Delete Forever"** to confirm

Your account and data are deleted immediately upon confirmation.

**Alternatively, by email:**
If you're unable to access the app, email paulchamp1602@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Account", sent from the email address associated with your account.

**Using PeakPilot as a guest (no account)?**
Guest data is stored against a random identifier on your device, not an email address, so there's no "Delete Account" option in the app for guests. To request deletion, email paulchamp1602@gmail.com and let us know you're a guest user — include any details that can help us identify your device (for example, information from app support logs), since we cannot look up guest data by name or email.

## What gets deleted
- Account details (email, display name)
- Workout history and streak data
- Saved favourite workouts
- Weekly workout plans

Deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no recovery period.

## What isn't deleted
Google Play, the Apple App Store, and RevenueCat keep their own independent records of your purchase and subscription history under their own retention policies. Deleting your PeakPilot account does not delete those records — contact Google Play, Apple, or RevenueCat directly for that. If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also revokes your Sign in with Apple authorization at the same time.
